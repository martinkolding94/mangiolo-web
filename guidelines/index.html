Community Guidelines

Mangiolo is a place to get curious about food — what people cook and what they eat. Keep it kind, keep it about food.

Do

  • Share what you cook and eat: recipes, meals at home, at restaurants or takeaway
  • Be generous: say what you liked, ask questions, share tips
  • Give credit when your recipe builds on someone else's — it's how we learn from each other
  • Use your own photos

Don't

  • Harass, bully, threaten or mock others — including their food
  • Post hate speech or content that targets people for who they are
  • Post sexual, violent or shocking content
  • Spam, advertise or post content that isn't about food
  • Pretend to be someone else or create fake accounts
  • Share dangerous advice, such as unsafe food practices
  • Post other people's personal information

If you see something wrong

Tap ••• and choose Report on a post, comment or profile. We review reports within 24 hours. You can also Block anyone — they won't see your posts, and you won't see theirs.

What happens if rules are broken

We remove the content. Depending on how serious it is: a warning, then a suspended account, then a permanent ban. Serious cases lead to an immediate ban.
